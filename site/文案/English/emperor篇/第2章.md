Sylvie's Father：Our invasion of the Human Empire is imminent. We must prepare ourselves to fight.  
Sylvie：We're invading the Human Empire... Am I allowed to go into battle, too?!  
Sylvie's Father：That, I do not know yet.  
Sylvie's Father：But invading the Human Empire has been a long-held wish of us Dark Realm dwellers.  
Sylvie's Father：So it's entirely feasible that a trainee knight like yourself could join us in battle.  
Sylvie：Yes, sir! May I bring this sword along?!  
Sylvie's Father：I doubt it can be of use to us now. Not even your mother or I can wield it well.  
Sylvie's Father：And it takes more than a good sword to fell a foe. It's a knight’s duty to choose a suitable weapon.  
Sylvie：Yes, sir...  
Sylvie's Father：Don't worry. You will master that sword soon. That is why you must survive this war.  
Sylvie：Understood! I promise I'll survive the war!  
Dark Sorcerer：Halt.  
Sylvie's Father：What?!  
Dark Sorcerer：Dark Knights are not permitted passage beyond this point.  
Sylvie's Father：Not permitted, you say?! But we are to see Lord Shasta and—  
Dark Sorcerer：Bah! How dare you speak the name of that filthy traitor.  
Sylvie's Mother：Traitor?! You bastard... How could you speak of Lord Shasta with such irreverence—  
Dark Sorcerer：Did you not hear what I said? That coward turned his sword on His Majesty.  
Sylvie：What?! He betrayed...the Emperor?!  
Dark Sorcerer：That's right, little wench. Ordinarily, this would be grounds to execute the all of the Dark Knights.  
Dark Sorcerer：Thank His Majesty's merciful heart that you still have your heads.  Now, leave my sight at once!  
Sylvie：B-But...you're mistaken, sir! Lord Shasta would never—  
Dark Sorcerer：Silence! Defy His Majesty and I'll—  
Sylvie's Father：Sylvie!  
Sylvie's Father：My sincerest apologies. We were misinformed. We shall leave at once, as per your command.  
Dark Sorcerer：Humph! Yes, get out of my sight, you rotten knight.  
Sylvie：......  
Sylvie's Mother：Let's go, Sylvie.  
Sylvie：Yes, Mother.

Sylvie's Father：People like you and I cannot fathom what lies in Lord Shasta's heart.  
Sylvie's Father：But perhaps, deep within him, there was a feeling far more precious to him than anything.  
Sylvie：A precious feeling? More precious than even the Law of Power?  
Sylvie's Father：As I said, people like you and I cannot fathom what lies in Lord Shasta's heart.  
Sylvie's Father：But I have faith that Lord Shasta is not a traitor. We haven't heard any of this from the Emperor either.  
Sylvie's Father：I'm certain that as Commander of the Dark Knights and as a man, there was something he could not compromise.  
Sylvie：Something he couldn't compromise...  
Sylvie's Father：You may not be capable of understanding yet.  
Sylvie：P-Please, Father! I am not a child!  
Sylvie's Father：Haha, sorry. But Sylvie... if you remember only one thing, remember this.  
Sylvie：Hm...?  
Sylvie's Father：You might encounter a difficult trial in the future.  
Sylvie's Father：And when that time comes, I want you to act in a way that won't shame you as a knight...or as a person.  
Sylvie：That won't shame me? I don't understand that word.  
Sylvie's Father：It means, to never be embarrassed by your deeds, and to live in a way that leaves you with no regrets.  
Sylvie：To never be embarrassed... So, do you mean you don't want anything I do to embarrass you or Mother?  
Sylvie's Father：Sure, you may think of it that way for now. When you get older, you will certainly understand.  
Sylvie：All right... I get it.  
Sylvie：But I would never do anything to embarrass you or Mother!  
Sylvie's Mother：I know. You have always been a very good girl, Sylvie.  
Sylvie：Thank you, Mother! And I'll always be good!  
Sylvie's Father：Yes, I know. Now, let's get back home and prepare for battle.  
Sylvie's Father：We want to be ready to sally forth on a moment's notice.  
Sylvie：I can't see the enemy or even the Human Empire... If only there were a clearly marked gate or something.  
Sylvie：I heard the earth split with a loud noise last night... I wonder if the Human Empire army caused it?  
Sylvie：If they really are capable of such mystical feats, then the front lines must have suffered heavy casualties.  
Sylvie：Unless it was the Emperor's doing? He is, after all, the god Vecta's second coming.  
Sylvie：Mother or Father would probably know, but being a trainee knight, I'm far back in the rearguard.  
Sylvie：I wonder how Mother and Father are doing... They should be all right, since there's no fighting for now.  
Sylvie：Still, I must reach the front as soon as possible. Otherwise, the war will end before I get a chance to fight.  
Sylvie：Even if we do make it across, I'll need a big win, otherwise the land will go to other clans.  
Sylvie：But if the Human Empire advances on us instead, that would mean we would be losing anyway...  
Commander：Order of the Dark Knights, assemble! We've received orders from His Majesty!  
Sylvie：Orders from His Majesty?! Do we get to go to the front lines, too?
