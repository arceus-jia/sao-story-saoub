## 场景 0001（未引用文本，按编号排列）


### Beta Test: Mito

Misumi：One day after class, I was struggling with a dilemma.  
Misumi：Somebody saw it... My secret. And it just had to be that top honors student, too.  
Misumi：I started to hear footsteps coming down the stairs. Footsteps that would seal my fate.  
Misumi：My entire future hangs on this moment!  
Asuna：Hello, Misumi.  
Misumi：Asuna Yuuki was a classmate I never really got along with.  
Misumi：Thanks for coming. I wanted to talk about—  
Asuna：What happened at the arcade yester- day, right? You were truly amazing! I couldn't believe what I saw!  
Misumi：That's right. Yesterday, I entered a tournament at an arcade by the train station.  
Misumi：And Asuna, who happened to be passing by, accidentally saw me there.  
Misumi：Since I was busy with the tourney, I asked her to wait for me, but she had to leave before it was over.  
Misumi：Which is why this matter must be resolved now.  
Misumi：We attend what you'd call a prep school for rich girls.  
Misumi：If the school found out I was at an arcade tournament, it would go on my permanent record. Or worse...  
Asuna：You were screaming, "Take that, ya bastard!" as you knocked down one strongman after another.  
Misumi：I wasn't screaming; I was roaring!  
Asuna：You were so cool, though... I wish I could be that good.  
Misumi：Huh? Wait... I thought you were going to scold me for corrupting my fragile mind with games.  
Asuna：But I'd never say anything like that! If anything, I envy you.  
Misumi：S-So...will you keep it a secret?  
Asuna：Of course I will!  
Misumi：Thanks. I owe you one...  
Asuna：Oh... Then, could you show me how you did it?  
Misumi：How I did what?  
Asuna：Aced that game! I've been so busy with studying and I really need a break.  
Misumi：Whatever floats your boat...  
Misumi：Oh! Actually, I'm playing this mobile game right now.  
Asuna：What's it like?  
Misumi：I installed the game on her phone and we played it together a little.  
Asuna：Wow, this game's fun! I like how you can show off with your attacks.  
Misumi：Yeah, it's even more fun when you win.  
Misumi：But you're messing up the commands. Slow down a little and be more deliberate.  
Asuna：Ah-hah... Guess I've got some studying to do... Thanks for the help!  
Misumi：Haha... You called it "studying." Yeah, let's meet up here again tomorrow.  
Misumi：Asuna walked away with a smile.  
Misumi：Hard to believe that the heiress to the megacorp Lect, Inc. was a gamer.  
Misumi：I always assumed a rich girl like her would have more high-class hobbies.  
Misumi：Hmm? I got a message...  
Email：Selection Results for the Sword Art Online Beta Test   
Misumi：Huh?!  
Misumi：Eee! They picked me!!! Woo-hoo!!!

Misumi：From the next day onward, Asuna and I would meet up after school to game together.  
Asuna：Okay, I'll use a combo and...  
Misumi：Not quite.  
Asuna：What?! Arrrgh...  
Asuna：I practiced so hard. Why can't I win?  
Misumi：It takes days of dedicated practice.  
Misumi：Well, good thing it's almost summer vacation. Want to meet up every day and play?  
Asuna：Sure! Oh, but I'll be studying in the mornings... How about for an hour or so in the afternoon?  
Misumi：Actually, afternoons aren't great for me...  
Asuna：Is there something you're working on?  
Misumi：Let's just say there's something I'm really excited about.  
Asuna：Okay. Then, could we meet up at 11am and play until the early afternoon?  
Misumi：That would work.  
Asuna：Okay, let's do that then.  
Mito：The Sword Art Online beta test was launched.  
Mito：And my heart sang with joy over being one of the privileged few who got to play it.  
Mito：The full-immersion VR world stunned me. The sheer depth of the system enchanted me.  
Mito：I picked a character name for myself: Mito.  
Mito：And I gave myself a gruff-looking face—an aesthetic I really like.  
Mito：I chose a male character for the challenge of role-playing the opposite gender.  
Mito：I took the game seriously, even from day one, because even though it was just a beta test... I wanted to shine.  
Mito：Every day, I logged in to the Town of Beginnings and grinded hard to level up.  
Mito：But I soon discovered that was a mistake...  
Mito：Why are there less monsters than yesterday?  
Player A：Hey, this's my hunting ground! Stay out!  
Player B：Hey, gimme a break! I can't find any monsters!  
Mito：Players started to fight over the depleted hunting grounds.  
Mito：Ah-hah. Now I get it.  
Mito：Small fights are meaningless here.  
Mito：In SAO, even the monsters you can hunt are finite.  
Mito：The devs might've built it so the front-runners get all the big wins.  
Mito：I can't be a top player if I keep hunting here. I need to move on.  
Mito：I left the wild prairie and trekked into the deep, thick forest...

Mito：(A village! I found a village!)  
Mito：(Okay, I'll use this as a home base while I look for a new place to hunt.)  
Mito：Wow. I can't believe there's a village in such a remote place...  
Mito：Huh? Is that...voices? They're coming from over there.  
Player C：I'm out! It ain't happening! Even if all four of us fight, it'll definitely be a TPK.  
Kirito：Come on, we'll be fine as long as long as we're not beaten black and blue.  
Player D：Yeah, but the bronze swords they sell in town break so easily.  
Kirito：That's why I keep saying we should use small blades.  
Player E：Yeah, well, we're off the quest. Have fun dying by yourself.  
Kirito：Fine...  
Mito：The other three players stormed off.  
Mito：All that remained was one dejected, lonely player.  
Kirito：Just when I finally find a party, I lose them. Being a solo player sure makes me feel helpless.  
Mito：Hey.  
Kirito：Whoa?! Hi, crazy-eyes!  
Mito：Gee, you're nice... Well, whatever. What happened?  
Kirito：I found a hidden quest. But the difficulty level is too high for me to clear it as a solo player.  
Mito：Yeah, the monsters around here are pretty powerful, compared to the ones in the plains.  
Kirito：I tried to form a party, but nobody stuck around.  
Mito：Guess you'll have to grind, rack up some XP, and try later.  
Kirito：But I'll miss my chance if I take too long.  
Kirito：I found an empty treasure chest yesterday. Somebody else took it.  
Kirito：In that moment, it all became clear to me.  
Kirito：To win this game, you have to take risks. Only a risk-taking front-runner can win.  
Mito：...!!  
Mito：It was like...he'd read my mind.  
Mito：That's why I'd gone into the forest. To come out ahead of all the other players.  
Mito：This guy's pretty cool. I think I'll team up with him.  
Mito：Hey... Could I go with you on this quest?  
Kirito：Would you?!  
Mito：Is a team of two enough to clear it?  
Kirito：It's going to be tricky... But as long as we're careful, we might be able to pull it off.  
Mito：Like, use lower rank weapons to prioritize our defense and make sure we're not beaten black and blue?  
Kirito：Oh, so you heard the plan? Cool. Come with me to this NPC's house. That's where we get the quest.  
Kirito：It's called "Forest Elixir." We're sup- posed to clear the area of monsters so we can forage some "ovules."  
Mito：Whoa, slow down there. I didn't understand a word you said.  
Kirito：Hahaha. Really?  
Mito：You're ridiculous...

Mito：I'm happy for you.

Mito：I'll just sell it to someone who wants it. See ya around.  
Kirito：Sure thing. Good luck to both of us. Oh, right! What's your name?  
Mito：Next time we meet, I'll tell you.  
Kirito：I wish you luck.  
Mito：Right back at ya.  
Mito：We parted ways with smiles on our faces. I made this village my home base and got to work leveling up.  
Mito：The only time I saw that unnamed swordsman again was from behind, just before the end of the beta test.  
Mito：We never learned each other's names.  
Mito：But I'm sure we'll meet again. When SAO goes live...
