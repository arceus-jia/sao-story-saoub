Rondo of a Fragile Blade: Part 4  
Wednesday, December 14, 2022  
The front-line players, including me and Asuna, finally reached the chamber of the second-floor boss.  
The raid party breakdown included Lind's blue group and Kibaou's green group, totaling eighteen each.  
Lind's group was called the Dragon Knights and Kibaou's was the Aincrad Liberation Squad.  
There were also the five members of the Legend Braves, Agil's four, and...  
...me and Asuna for forty-seven total.  
Too bad we're just one person short of a full raid.  
Yes, it is a shame. I guess he didn't make it in time.  
Well, we did get here ahead of schedule. It's a tough quest to beat in just three days.  
From what I hear, even a certain someone took three days and two nights to beat it.  
Three days ago, the last time we saw Nezha, I gave him a special ranged weapon and a map.  
The map led to the master who had taught me the Martial Art skill.  
The circumstances leading to me gaining the Martial Art skill were a little complicated...  
But I'll have to leave that story for another day.  
I asked Nezha if he was ready to trade the weapon-crafting skill he'd worked so hard on for martial arts.  
The rare weapon I'd obtained required both the Martial Art skill and the Throwing Knives skill.  
It's tough to abandon a skill you've worked on, but it's risky to try to multiclass when you're in the field.  
Which was why I dared to encourage Nezha to drop his Blacksmith skill.  
And at my suggestion, which some would have called cruel, Nezha just took a deep breath and agreed.  
As long as I can be a swordsman here, I don't need anything else.  
Though I don't suppose I could call myself a swordsman, with a weapon like this.  
Everyone who's fighting to clear this game is a swordsman. Even a pure crafter.  
If he had finished his martial arts training on time, we could have asked him to join the raid.  
But it seemed three days wasn't enough for him to break that rock.  
But there was no need to rush. Nezha wouldn't be risking danger by trying his upgrade fraud again.  
I'm sure he'll join us when we raid the third floor.  
His weapon is a pretty good one if he can master it, so he'll be able to find a spot in some guild.  
One aside from the Braves, I'm guessing...  
Yes...I hope so.  
The Braves look a bit jumpy. Maybe they're nervous because of Nezha's sudden disappearance.  
Sure, but it's not our job to calm their nerves.  
That aside, Kirito...  
We shouldn't be spending our time worrying about the state of the other parties.  
Oh? Why?  
Lind said we'd put the raid group together just before the boss fight, but think about it.  
There are three parties for the blue team, three parties for the green team, one for the Braves...  
...and probably one last one for Agil's group. That makes eight.  
Oh. G-Good point. I hadn't thought about it, but eight parties is the maximum for a raid.
