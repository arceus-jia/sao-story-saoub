## 场景 0001（未引用文本，按编号排列）


### Beta Test: Kirito

Kazuto Kirigaya：I was sitting in my room, waiting for the moment to arrive.  
Kazuto Kirigaya：I kept glancing at the clock, watching the start-time creep closer and closer.  
Kazuto Kirigaya：And in between glances at my clock, my eyes always returned to my headgear, sitting on the shelf.  
Kazuto Kirigaya：It's called a NerveGear.  
Kazuto Kirigaya：At age seven, I abandoned the way of the sword—kendo—to serve my computer overlords.  
Kazuto Kirigaya：I was such a nerd that I even built a PC from scratch in elementary school.  
Kazuto Kirigaya：The electronic world filled my life with joy...  
Kazuto Kirigaya：...but this virtual world was the real deal. It would make all my dreams and visions come true.  
Kazuto Kirigaya：And today, this NerveGear...  
Kazuto Kirigaya：...would take me to the new world of Sword Art Online in its beta test.  
Kazuto Kirigaya：Is that footsteps? Who could it be.  
Suguha Kirigaya：Kirito...  
Kazuto Kirigaya：Oh, Sugu. It's just you.  
Suguha Kirigaya：Mom and Dad told me why they got mad last night. Did you really ditch your parent-teacher conference?  
Kazuto Kirigaya：Yeah.  
Suguha Kirigaya：You said you were gonna go. Why'd you lie?  
Kazuto Kirigaya：Well...I had reasons.  
Suguha Kirigaya：Reasons?  
Kazuto Kirigaya：Yeah. Personal reasons...  
Suguha Kirigaya：Mom said she was worried about all your absences, too.  
Kazuto Kirigaya：I looked at the clock then, and noticed it was almost time.  
Kazuto Kirigaya：Sorry, are you done?  
Suguha Kirigaya：But Kiri... Yeah.  
Kazuto Kirigaya：After Suguha left, I put the Nerve- Gear on my head and lied down on my bed.  
Kazuto Kirigaya：Five minutes until launch...  
Kazuto Kirigaya：Forget about reality. Just let go...  
Kazuto Kirigaya：The VR world the NerveGear lets you enter is not the dream or fantasy you might think it is.  
Kazuto Kirigaya：And I was well aware that it's only a tool that helps me temporarily forget what a loser Kazuto Kirigaya is.  
Kazuto Kirigaya：But that vivid, novel world was calling to me like a siren...and I was thirsty for it.  
Kazuto Kirigaya：No matter what sort of destruction awaited me on the other side...  
Kazuto Kirigaya：...I'd happily drown in the glorious ocean that is "Full Dive."  
Kazuto Kirigaya：The launch clock started counting down. I took a deep breath.  
Kazuto Kirigaya：Okay. Time for the beta test. Link Start...
