Diavel：No, I came to see if the rumors were true. And to get a little preview, while I was at it.  
Mito：What rumors? And what preview?  
Diavel：Two questions, I see...  
Diavel：I'll answer the latter first. I came to get a preview of the Boss Room.  
Mito：Did you already go there?  
Diavel：Yes, a little while ago. Now, as for the rumors...  
Diavel：Word has it there's this powerful solo player.  
Mito：Sounds interesting. Tell me more.  
Diavel：She's a rarity—a lady who fights with a scythe. And apparently, she does live up to her reputation.  
Mito：Does anyone else besides me use a scythe?  
Diavel：That's the main reason I came to this labyrinth—to find out.  
Diavel：Actually, I came here to see you.  
Mito：Me? But...why? Aren't there stronger players than me?  
Mito：When I asked him this, Diavel got a look in his eye.  
Diavel：I did encounter one solo player of a higher level...  
Diavel：But he's a special case.  
Diavel：By the way, would you like to come to the boss raid meeting?  
Mito：What's that?  
Diavel：A gathering of everyone here, to decide how we're going to defeat the boss of the first floor.  
Mito：Ah... The circumstances we're facing are very different from the beta test, after all.  
Diavel：Ah-hah. So you were a beta tester.  
Mito：Yeah...  
Diavel：I want to help everyone get back to the real world. Will you help us?  
Mito：......  
Mito：After what I'd gone through, I should have been just as determined as he was.  
Mito：I should have been right on board... but I just didn't have it in me.  
Mito：After all, it was a mistake in a party that caused it. The incident that made me lose my motivation.  
Mito：It's a hell I didn't ever want to experience again.  
Mito：So I thought it would be best if I went at it alone—if I didn't get attached to anyone.  
Mito：Well, I...  
Mito：I wanted to turn him down, but my voice froze.  
Mito：Because I knew in my heart that I was limited as a solo player.  
Mito：If I wanted to keep my lead ahead of the other players, I'd need to team up with someone eventually.  
Mito：It's a reality I couldn't escape. I had to overcome my trauma and choose the best option.  
Mito：Okay... I'll go to the meeting.

Diavel：We're taking an awfully long time on this first floor boss fight.  
Diavel：Do you know why that is?  
Mito：Yeah... Because we're terrified.  
Diavel：SAO is now a death game. Those on the front lines will be staring death right in the face.  
Diavel：And where is the likelihood of death the highest? Boss fights.  
Diavel：I have my concerns too. But given our current situation, I decided to reassess my way of thinking.  
Diavel：We need all the fighters we can get. Am I wrong?  
Mito：......  
Diavel：I nipped their conspiracy theories in the bud once. I can do it again.  
Diavel：Problem solved. Right?  
Mito：Wow... You're really mature.  
Diavel：Funny, you don't seem that immature to me.  
Mito：Hahaha.  
Diavel：Good. Keep smiling. You have a beautiful smile.  
Mito：What?!  
Mito：Haha, very funny. Well, bye.
