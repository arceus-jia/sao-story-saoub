## 场景 0001（未引用文本，按编号排列）


### The Secret of the Baltio Style

Golgorosso：Hmmm...  
Eugeo：H-how was I?  
Golgorosso：Good. Nice and strong. You've got great muscles!  
Eugeo：Ouch! Uh, thank you!  
Golgorosso：I hear you've been training outside the school practice rooms. Here. Eat up.  
Eugeo：Huh? May I?  
Golgorosso：Eating is part of training. You can't build up big muscles without a lot of fuel.  
Eugeo：Aye! Then I'll gladly eat!  
Golgorosso：Good! Eat up!  
Golgorosso：But I am surprised... In three months of training, you never once complained.  
Golgorosso：And here I thought I was training you pretty hard.  
Eugeo：You have been, Sir! However, I am so honored that you would train me that I...  
Golgorosso：Enough with the "Aye!" and "Sir," kiddo. Training hours are over. Relax a little.  
Eugeo：Aye!   
Eugeo：But honestly, I didn't think your training was too strict.  
Golgorosso：Probably because you've already had a lot of strict training.  
Golgorosso：Hehe. I've got a good eye for this sort of thing.  
Eugeo：The strict training...I've already had? But I was merely carrying out my Calling.  
Golgorosso：Which means you've managed to change your Calling once. That is impressive.  
Eugeo：Oh, don't praise me... I didn't accomplish it alone. Kirito helped a lot.  
Golgorosso：But it's still impressive. What was your former Calling?  
Eugeo：I was a "Carver." I chopped a giant tree with an axe.  
Golgorosso：An axe...now that makes sense.  
Eugeo：What makes sense?  
Golgorosso：Your muscles. They aren't the muscles of a nobleman who's swung only a sword.  
Golgorosso：I've been curious all this time as to where your muscles came from.  
Eugeo：You're right. These muscles may not be well-suited to Sword Arts. But I still...  
Golgorosso：Not well-suited to Sword Arts? No, you've got that backwards. Your muscles are perfect.  
Eugeo：Are you sure?  
Golgorosso：The moment your axe hits the tree is the most important, right? Otherwise, the tree won't fall.  
Eugeo：Yes. That is true.  
Golgorosso：Fencing uses the same principle. You need to put force into your opponent when you hit him.  
Golgorosso：Your years of axe-swinging were truly a great treasure, Eugeo.  
Eugeo：Axe-swinging was...a treasure?  
Golgorosso：That's right. So have confidence. With a little confidence, you will grow even stronger.  
Eugeo：Y-yes, Sir! Thank you, Mentor Golgorosso!  
Golgorosso：Good. Now you look energized. I did say training is done, but do you want to go another round?  
Eugeo：Yes, please!

Eugeo：I do... I owe my life to him. He helped me get this far.  
Eugeo：But he's been getting even stronger under Liena's training.  
Eugeo：I'm worried that he's overtaking me... Oh, exuse me! I'm rambling.  
Golgorosso：Don't worry about it. Listening to my page's woes is my duty as your trainer.  
Golgorosso：Eugeo...do you want to overtake Kirito?  
Eugeo：Yes... Someday, I have to be able to defeat him.  
Golgorosso：I see...  
Golgorosso：As for me, I care more about competing against my own strength, not against someone else.  
Golgorosso：Or, rather, maybe my true hope is simply to win. Probably because I used to be a guard.  
Eugeo：You were with the guard?  
Golgorosso：Yes. A guard's duty is to protect the people. So being stronger than other guards is not necessary.  
Eugeo：That's true... I think it's a noble Calling.  
Golgorosso：Yes. I am proud of it. But that is also why I'll never hold a candle to Volo or Liena.  
Golgorosso：Whenever I watch them fight, I can't help but be awestruck. They have trained so well.  
Golgorosso：I can't beat them, the way I am now, and I know it. That's my nature.  
Eugeo：I'm sure it's probably the same with me. Whenever I watch Kirito fight, I'm filled with respect.  
Golgorosso：But you have one thing you can't compromise.  
Eugeo：Sure...  
Eugeo：That's why I'm here. I need to be able to beat Kirito.  
Golgorosso：Understood. In that case, you need to train to "win."  
Golgorosso：I’ll up your training starting tomorrow. I'll teach you the "secret side" of Baltio Style.  
Eugeo：The...secret side?  
Golgorosso：Yes. Moves for fighting in narrow spaces, in the dark, or when under siege.  
Golgorosso：To be honest, these moves will be useless in Academy duels.  
Golgorosso：In school matches, you never intentionally get behind your opponent. Still want to learn?  
Eugeo：Yes, Sir! Please, I want to learn the secrets of your Baltio Style!  
Golgorosso：Good. Well, brace yourself. It's gonna hurt a lot.  
Eugeo：I can hardly wait!
