### Memories of Bygone Days: Bercouli

Bercouli：Good work today, Fanatio.  
Fanatio：I live to serve you, Commander! I only hope my incompetence didn't cause you too much pain...  
Bercouli：Oh, it's only a small burn. Once we get back to the Cathedral, I’ll be good as new.  
Fanatio：Please, sir, don't overexert yourself. Remember, you just defeated quite the powerful enemy.  
Bercouli：Yeah, it was pretty powerful. And I didn't expect it to come back to life after we killed it.  
Bercouli：Her Eminence said it was a phoenix...and it definitely lived up to its horrifying reputation.  
Fanatio：Indeed worthy of the title of divine beast. Though no match for you or Her Eminence, of course.  
Bercouli：Whoa, now. Don't put Her Eminence down on my level.  
Fanatio：M-My apologies!  
Bercouli：Well, nevertheless, we put it down for good this time, so I doubt it'll come back to life again.  
Fanatio：True... And I'd like to think that we shan't see a fight as grueling as that ever again.  
Bercouli：You said it.  
Bercouli：Well, let's get back to the Cathedral so we can get some rest.  
Fanatio：Yes, sir!  
Bercouli：Hm...? What's that?  
Fanatio：Something wrong, Commander?  
Bercouli：Fanatio. Look over there.  
Fanatio：Over...where, sir?  
Bercouli：See that mountain to the right? At the foot of it.  
Fanatio：The foot of the mount... Ah!  
Fanatio：There are people climbing up the mountain! Despite how steep it is?  
Bercouli：Yeah, that concerns me, too. And it's pretty far from the nearest village.  
Bercouli：Why would they want to leave their homes and go into the mountain?  
Fanatio：It's hard to imagine an attack came from the Dark Territory. I don't think there's any danger.  
Bercouli：Right? I was thinking the same thing.  
Bercouli：Were they out getting fruit or were they hunting? Either or, it doesn't add up.  
Fanatio：Commander, what should we do?  
Bercouli：It doesn't appear that they violated the Taboo Index, but let's go take a look to be sure.  
Fanatio：Yes, sir!
