Alice：If you are saying all of this in earnest, then I...

Alice：You'd better not mention things like an all-out attack around anyone besides me.  
Eydis：Yeah, I'll be good. Sorry about that, Alice.

Eydis：I'd wanna snuggle with my Alice!  
Eydis：I'd want to find whatever looks fun and live life freely on my own terms.

Eydis：Oh! Wait, Alice!  
Alice：Live freely on your own terms... Hehe. Yes, that sounds very much like you, Eydis.  
Eydis：Well yeah, we've been so focused on the Dark Realm that we know far too little of this world.  
Eydis：What would you do, Alice?  
Alice：Let me think... Oh! I'd learn to cook.  
Eydis：Cook?  
Alice：Yes... Deep in my heart, I've always had the desire to cook delicious food.  
Eydis：Ooh. Cook delicious food, eh?  
[1]：I like it! And I'd love to try your cooking, too, Alice.

Eydis：Cooking? Sounds like a hassle. I'm much more a fan of eating.

Eydis：Oh! That's not what I meant...  
Alice：Believing the wars will end someday is a bit naive anyway.  
Eydis：Alice, listen...  
Alice：I'll take my leave now, Eydis.  
Alice：M-My cooking?! Well, if I ever get good enough at it...  
Eydis：Maybe I should learn to cook, too. Yeah, then we could cook together!  
Eydis：Hehe, I can picture us now, side by side in the kitchen... That would be lovely.  
Alice：Lovely? Yes... I guess it would.  
Eydis：You said it! Hehe, now I'm really excited about it!  
[1]：Where would we cook? The Cathedral kitchens, right? They do have a lot of spices.  
[2]：Where would we cook? I'm thinking some quiet place outside the city.  
Eydis：Where would we cook? The Cathedral kitchens, right? They do have a lot of spices.  
Eydis：Where would we cook? I'm thinking some quiet place outside the city.  
Alice：The Cathedral kitchens? That could work...  
Alice：But if the wars really did end, I think I'd want to move away from the Cathedral.  
Eydis：Huh? Really?! But living here is so convenient!  
Alice：True, it's easier to care for our dragons here, and there's an endless supply of weapons.  
Alice：But if there is no war in this theoretical future...then I'd want to live somewhere quiet.  
Eydis：Quiet, eh?  
Alice：Naturally, as long as I'm in the Human Empire, I will dedicate my life to my mission.  
Alice：We should get going, Eydis... The knights should be returning from their expeditions soon.  
Alice：You think so too, Eydis?! Yes, I was just thinking about how I want to live in the countryside.  
Eydis：Ooh, really?! I guess we're peas in a pod, you and I!  
Alice：Peas and pods aside... I really do love the idea of cooking in a quiet cottage.  
Alice：Ideally, this cottage would be in a lush forest so our dragons could live with us.  
Eydis：Ooh, yes, I love that! There's plenty of yummy fruits and vegetables to forage, too.  
Alice：Yes, truly... If only such a day would come.  
Eydis：I'm sure it will come. Just work hard now and don't give up hope.  
Alice：Yes, you're absolutely right, Eydis.  
Alice：If a heavenly life is waiting for me, I'll be more than happy to perform my duties.  
Eydis：You said it! Hehe...I'm in so happy.  
Alice：You're happy? Why?  
Eydis：Because you and I have so much in common. No mistaking it, we're definitely sisters.  
Alice：I don't think you're quite right, but...I appreciate the sentiment, Eydis.  
Eydis：Oh, oh...Alice...  
[1]：Alice, I love you!  
[2]：Let's work hard today, so that we might have that future.  
Eydis：Alice, I love you!  
Eydis：Let's work hard today, so that we might have that future.  
Alice：Eydis! Hey, not there...  
Eydis：My Alice!  
Alice：Please, get a hold of yourself! Goodness, I can never let my guard down around you.  
Eydis：But I'm just so happy you feel the same way as I do!  
Alice：That's not a valid excuse! Come on, please... Learn to show a little restraint!  
Alice：Hey... P-Please... I'm not a child anymore.  
Eydis：Haha. But you'll always be my precious baby sister.  
Alice：......  
Eydis：I know! Why don't we ask Chef Hana to teach us how to cook something simple?  
Alice：What...you mean right now? But...  
Eydis：It's never too early to start learning to cook.  
Eydis：We can learn together. Please, Alice?  
Alice：Haha, oh, I can never say no to you when you get like that. Okay, I'll come with you.  
Eydis：Yaaay! To the kitchen, Alice!

Alice：By command of the High Pontifex herself, the water is impeccably pure and abundant.  
Kirito：Pure and abundant water...  
Alice：I seem to recall you were sweaty.  
Kirito：Yeah, because I had to lift you all the way up the Cathedral walls!  
Alice：I didn't ask for that.  
[1]：Jeez, at least say thank you.  
[2]：I should've just left you behind.  
Kirito：Jeez, at least say thank you.  
Kirito：I should've just left you behind.

Kirito：Hey, that's not what I really...  
Alice：I was just starting to trust you, but you are, after all, a spy that sneaked into the Cathedral.  
Alice：I almost let myself forget that.  
Alice：From now on, I must make certain I don't let my guard down around you.
