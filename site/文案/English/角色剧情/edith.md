Mary：Me too.

Mary：It really was tasty.

Eydis：I think flopping in bed right after a satisfying meal would really hit the spot.

Mary：No, I have to wash up.  
Eydis：Aww, come on!

Eydis：Argh... Well, if I must.  
Eydis：I suppose you're right.
